# Momentum privacy policy

*Effective October 20, 2026. Published at https://paul-blake.com/momentum/privacy (`pages/momentum/privacy.js` in the website repo); keep the two in step.*

Momentum is a habit tracker. It was made to keep your habits private, and it has nothing to sell: no ads, no accounts, no tracking.

## The short version

- **Nothing you put in Momentum leaves your phone.** It has no servers and no accounts, and it doesn't send your habits or Health data anywhere.
- **No ads, analytics or tracking.** No one is watching how you use it, including us.

## What Momentum keeps, and where

**Kept on your device:**
- your habits, the days you log, notes, quotes you save or write, categories, achievements and settings;
- for habits you link to Apple Health, the latest numbers read from Health, so the app can show them without asking again.

**Who can see it:** only Momentum and its widgets on your phone.

**Backups:** iOS includes this data in your phone's backups (iCloud or your computer), if you've turned those on, the same as other apps' data. Momentum doesn't make copies of its own.

**Deleting it:** deleting the app deletes this data.

## Apple Health

You choose whether to link a habit to Apple Health, and which data Momentum may use. You can change this at any time in the Health app (Profile → Apps → Momentum).

- **Reading:** steps, exercise minutes, workouts, sleep, mindful minutes and water, to fill in habits you've linked.
- **Writing:** water and mindful minutes, only when you log them in a linked habit.
- **Never:** Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone. It isn't sent anywhere, since Momentum has no servers.

## Notifications

Reminders and streak warnings are scheduled on your phone by Momentum itself. They don't go through any server.

## Sharing

When you share a card (an achievement, a streak, a quote), Momentum makes a picture on your phone and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes is up to you. Momentum doesn't keep a copy or see where you sent it.

## Links

Momentum opens a web page only when you tap a link (for example "Buy me a coffee", where it's shown). That page belongs to its own site, and that site's privacy policy applies there.

## Children

Momentum is suitable for everyone and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Since Momentum collects nothing, any change that would collect something would be stated plainly in the app first.
